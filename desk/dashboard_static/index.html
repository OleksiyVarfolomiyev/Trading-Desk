      Market making (ladders)

      The ladders' own trading (c248, and VVV in 07f2). Ladder PnL is their result against simply holding the position you opened: spread captured (round trips) plus inventory drift (what the unmatched buys or sells made as the price moved) less fees. Markouts: the move 1 and 15 minutes after each fill, for (+) or against (−) it, in bps over the last 3 days; negative means the price runs through the quotes. Status says when the trims pause because the price trends the position's way. Position vs base: the band is in ladder.json (±25%). Quotes rest on HL's book; Queued are held in memory until the price nears them.

      Results by strategy

      HL positions and the last 30 days' fills, by the journal's tag: directional, conviction, hedge, and the ladders' market making. The market-making row gets only the ladders' own result against holding (as in the table above); the profit a ladder realizes by trimming a position stays with that position's strategy. Fills from before the journal began are untagged.

      Short candidates (screener)

      The 40 most liquid HL perps ranked by the signals the research harness validated (or the desk's own record until one is). Size at budget is what the 5% risk budget buys at the 3 × ATR stop; nothing trades until you run it.

      Market-making candidates

      Liquid coins that have gone nowhere over 30 days (low efficiency: net move against the sum of daily moves), so ladder quotes on both sides keep filling.

      Risk metrics

Current regime: realized daily returns since the book took its present shape; Sharpe and alpha appear after 40 days. Nothing is pooled across regimes. VaR is a one-day bootstrap loss on today's value. Updated hourly.
